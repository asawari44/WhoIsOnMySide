Narendra Modi – Selected Speeches and Statements on Caste, Untouchability, and Social Equality
Compiled from verified news archives and official government sources.
Prime Minister of India (in office since May 26, 2014).

Only direct quotes and closely paraphrased summaries from cited sources are included.

===========================================================================
Address at RSS (Rashtriya Swayamsevak Sangh) Centenary Celebrations
New Delhi, October 2025
Source: ProKerala, Greater Kashmir (pib.gov.in PRID 2173507)
===========================================================================

For centuries, social ills such as caste discrimination and untouchability have been challenges for Hindu society. These deep-rooted social ailments have long posed a serious challenge to the unity of Hindu society.

Every member of the Sangh, every Sarsanghchalak, has fought against such discrimination. From Dr. Hedgewar to the present day, every eminent figure and Sarsanghchalak of the Sangh has fought against discrimination and untouchability.

Pujya Balasaheb Deoras ji gave the call: "If untouchability is not a sin, then nothing in the world is." The present Sarsanghchalak has given a clear call for unity, calling for one well, one temple, one cremation ground for everyone.

Na Hindu Patito Bhavet — no Hindu can ever fall.

===========================================================================
Address at Inauguration of Dr. Ambedkar National Memorial
26 Alipur Road, New Delhi, April 2018
Source: India TV News
===========================================================================

We do injustice to Babasaheb by portraying him only as the messiah of Dalits. He was someone who raised the voice for all the downtrodden and suppressed people.

Babasaheb was the voice of the marginalised. He is a Vishwa Manav.

The way Sardar Patel worked for political unification, Baba Saheb Ambedkar worked for social unification. Baba Saheb was as iconic as Martin Luther King who fought for the oppressed.

When the issue of equal rights to women came up, Babasaheb was clear that if women do not get equal rights, I cannot be a part of the ministry.

Nothing has ever happened to reservation for Dalits, tribals, where we are in power. But still this lie is spread to mislead. This is right — nobody can snatch from Dalits, tribals and marginalised communities.

We strengthened the law against atrocities in 2015. We increased the list of punishable offences from 22 crimes to 47. We retained the non-bailable provision for the accused.

Parties who believe in Babasaheb Ambedkar should never be with the Congress. Our government has honoured Dalit icon Babasaheb Ambedkar as no other government has.

===========================================================================
Address to the Nation from the Red Fort – 68th Independence Day
August 15, 2014
Source: Gulf News (text of speech)
===========================================================================

The day of independence is a festival when we take a solemn pledge of working for the welfare of mother India, and also for the welfare of the poor, oppressed, dalits, the exploited and the backward people of our country.

Even after Independence, we have had to face the poison of casteism and communalism. Whether it is the poison of casteism, communalism, regionalism, discrimination on social and economic basis — all these are obstacles in our way forward.

Let us resolve, for once in our hearts, let us put a moratorium on all such activities for ten years. We shall march ahead to a society which will be free from all such tensions.

May 23, 2019
Source: The Caravan
===========================================================================

Only two castes will remain in this country. And the country is going to be focused on only these two castes. The first caste in India is the poor. And the second caste is of people who contribute whatever little to free the country from poverty.

===========================================================================
Campaign Rally, Kannauj, Uttar Pradesh
Source: The Caravan
===========================================================================

Meri jati itni choti hai, itni choti hai, gaon mein ekak ghar bhi nahi hota. Aur main toh pichda nahi, ati pichde barg mein paida hua.

[Translation: My caste is so small, so small that only a few from the community lived in the village. And I was born not only in a backward class, but in an extremely backward class.]

I have risen from a socially backward class, so my politics would be lowly politics for those people. But the selflessness, sacrifice and courage of the lower castes play a crucial role in uplifting the country to such heights.

Rajya Sabha Speech – Motion of Thanks to President's Address
February 2024
Source: The Wire
===========================================================================

Nowadays I think of Nehru quite a bit.

[Nehru wrote:] "I dislike any kind of reservation, particularly in jobs and services. I am strongly against anything which leads to inefficiency and second rate standards."

This is why I say that they are against reservation by birth. Nehru used to say that if SC/ST/OBC get reservations in jobs then the standard of government work will fall.

Congress and its allies have never given rights to SC/ST/OBC. Babasaheb's thoughts and politics were discarded by them.

Sabka Saath, Sabka Vikas is not a slogan — it is Modi's guarantee.

Our government would not allow the SC/ST law to get affected. We have consistently protected and strengthened the legal rights of Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes against caste-based atrocities and discrimination.
